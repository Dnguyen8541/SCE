# Lessons Learned

## Aircraft Gate Compatibility Validation
Operational simulations shall be reviewed throughout design development. Compliance alone does not guarantee gate functionality.

## NFPA 415 Grading Deficiency
Legacy apron grading directed fuel spills toward the building. Final solution used retaining walls, revised drainage, and a negotiated 0.75% slope within first 50 feet due to utility and operational constraints.

## Operational Validation
Review aircraft envelopes, service operations, vehicle circulation, and future fleet compatibility at every major milestone.
