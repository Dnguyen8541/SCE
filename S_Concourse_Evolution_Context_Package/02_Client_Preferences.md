# Client Preferences

## Capacity Preservation
Maintain gate count and operational capacity whenever practical.

## Aircraft Compatibility
Compatibility shall be demonstrated through analysis, not assumed.

## Future Fleet
Support Boeing 777-9 and Airbus A350-1000 operations.

## Operational Flexibility
Preserve airline assignment flexibility and future adaptability.

## Performance Over Perfection
Operational success may be more important than strict geometric perfection in constrained legacy environments.
