# Aircraft Operations Design Principles

## Design Aircraft
- Boeing 777-9
- Airbus A350-1000

## Software
AviPlan Airside Pro

## Gate Success Criteria
- Safe parking
- Efficient servicing
- Comparable or improved turn times
- Future fleet accommodation

## Critical Constraints
- PLB extension and compaction limits
- Ground service vehicle circulation
- Cargo dolly staging limitations
- Wingtip separation

## Stakeholders
Most involved airlines:
- Delta Air Lines
- Alaska Airlines
