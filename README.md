# S Concourse Evolution Knowledge Repository

## Purpose

This repository serves as the authoritative engineering knowledge base for the **S Concourse Evolution Program** at **Seattle\-Tacoma International Airport (SEA)**.

The repository captures:

- Program context
- Design standards
- Operational requirements
- Design decisions
- Historical lessons learned
- QA/QC methodology
- Aircraft operations criteria
- Construction phasing rationale
- Stakeholder requirements

The objective is to preserve institutional knowledge throughout design, construction, commissioning, and long\-term operations.

# Repository Philosophy

This repository is not intended to be a file archive.

The repository exists to answer two questions:

## 1\. What did the team decide?

Examples:

- Why were specific aircraft layouts selected?
- Why was a grading compromise accepted?
- Why were phasing approaches chosen?
- Why were certain standards interpreted differently?

## 2\. What should future teams learn?

Examples:

- What mistakes were made?
- What risks were discovered?
- What assumptions proved incorrect?
- What decisions were successful?

# Critical Design Drivers

The following design drivers shall be considered during all reviews.

## Aircraft Compatibility

Critical aircraft:

- Boeing 777\-9
- Airbus A350\-1000

Aircraft compatibility must be demonstrated, not assumed.

---

## Operational Functionality

The project shall support efficient:

- Aircraft movement
- Aircraft servicing
- Passenger boarding
- Ground support operations
- Airline operations

---

## Construction Phasing

Construction phasing is a core design component.

The airport must remain operational during construction.

Designs that cannot be reasonably phased should be reconsidered.

---

## Capacity Preservation

S Concourse supports a significant portion of SEA's international widebody operations.

Gate availability and capacity impacts should be evaluated during all design reviews.

---

## NFPA 415 Compliance

Apron grading and fuel spill management are primary safety drivers.

The intent of NFPA requirements should be achieved even where existing conditions prevent ideal geometric compliance.

---

# Lessons from Previous Programs

## International Arrivals Facility (IAF)

Key takeaway:

Compliance does not automatically produce operational success.

All gate layouts should be validated through operational analysis.

Review:

- Aircraft envelopes
- Wingtip separations
- Vehicle circulation
- Passenger boarding bridge performance
- Serviceability

at every major design milestone.

# AI Review Instructions

When using AI tools to support project reviews:

1. Review Context files first.
2. Review project documents second.
3. Prioritize aircraft operations over drafting perfection.
4. Evaluate operational functionality in addition to compliance.
5. Consider capacity preservation impacts.
6. Identify lessons learned.
7. Generate proposed repository updates.

The AI should function as a reviewer familiar with the history, constraints, operational objectives, and lessons learned associated with S Concourse Evolution.
